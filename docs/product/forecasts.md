# Forecasts

Short-interval previews are available for the next **5, 15 and 30 minutes**, requiring **10, 30 and 60 minutes** of history respectively. The page starts at 5 minutes. These use equally weighted 30-second means and recent lookbacks of 30 minutes, one hour and two hours respectively. At least 80% interval occupancy, no gaps over two minutes and the existing freshness/variability checks are required. Short projections are labeled experimental previews and do not establish planning accuracy. The hourly modes below retain their original requirements.

The Forecasts page projects CPU and memory for the cluster, a node, or a workload from the existing volatile history API. It reads up to 24 hours independently of the dashboard time filter. Namespace selection limits workload choices; cluster and node readings remain cluster-wide. Opening the selected resource reuses the connected investigation workspace.

The browser fits a linear trend to equal-weight five-minute sample means. A 1/3/6-hour horizon requires at least 2/6/12 hours of usable history, 80% occupied five-minute intervals, no gap greater than 15 minutes, and a measurement within 90 seconds. Highly variable series (residual RMS greater than half the mean), incomplete discovery, truncation, connection errors and missing current metrics suppress projections. Repeated timestamps are deduplicated; foreign UIDs and invalid values are excluded. Missing history is never filled with zero.

The envelope is heuristic: the larger of twice the fit RMS or the latest observation's deviation from the fitted value, multiplied by `1 + horizon / observed span`. Values and lower bounds are clipped at zero. It is not a calibrated confidence interval, a probability of failure or an outage prediction. Historical aggregates can reflect changing resource membership or partial collection; historical membership coverage is not persisted. Deployments, scaling, restarts and traffic changes can invalidate extrapolation. Calibration/backtesting remains future work.

The graph distinguishes observed history, dashed projections and a shaded range. A native range control supports keyboard/touch inspection of projected timestamps, values and bounds. At narrow widths the chart can scroll horizontally rather than shrinking labels. History age, sample count, occupied intervals and global evictions are visible. The in-memory global 60,000-point ceiling can shorten the 24-hour maximum; restarting the backend clears history.

No model request, AI-written explanation, new collector, database, backend endpoint or cluster permission is added. The page performs one bounded history query per selected UID per snapshot refresh (normally ten seconds) and computes the fit locally. This is an early trend projection feature, not full M3 anomaly detection or validated forecasting accuracy.
